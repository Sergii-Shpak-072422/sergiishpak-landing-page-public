Selected evidence

Selected work

SAGRO, Denmark

Workforce time-tracking platform

Owned the replacement of an abandoned legacy application across product definition, UX/UI, architecture, implementation, QA, release, and production iteration. At the time of the system's 2026 sale, SAGRO reported approximately 1,700 users across 250 agricultural businesses.

HortiScience Innovation Center, Netherlands

Agrifood innovation operations platform

Replaced an inherited platform that did not fit HIC's operating model with a custom Next.js and Supabase product, leading discovery, domain modelling, UX/UI, architecture, and hands-on delivery. The first production release was delivered in approximately one month; the platform is now used daily, and moving voucher administration out of Excel is a stakeholder-reported major time saver.

't Aailand, Netherlands

Subscription and delivery operating system

Turned high-level requirements into a production replacement spanning web, iOS, Android, and backend workflows. Mobile self-service eliminated manual new-customer onboarding and reduced email traffic; the system supports an estimated 400+ subscribers.

Additional work includes product extension, mobile UX, and migration foundations for Lucy Analytics in Sweden.
